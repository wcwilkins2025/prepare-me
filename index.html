Prepare Me

A plain check of where your household stands for the disasters that can reach your town. Then the next thing to fix, one step at a time. Free steps first.

How it works

  1. 1Enter your town and answer a few plain questions about your home and family. About five minutes.
  2. 2Prepare Me looks up the disasters FEMA counts in your county: the more likely ones and the less likely ones.
  3. 3You get a score for water, food, power and heat, medical, communications, a family plan, home, skills and money.
  4. 4Then one next step at a time. Who does it, what to do, when, why, and what done looks like. Steps that cost nothing come first.
  5. 5A short check-in each week shows how far you have come.

No fear and no shopping list. No store names. Just where you stand.
