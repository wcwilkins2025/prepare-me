Survival handbook

Every guide in Prepare Me, in one book you can print. Pick all of it or only the parts you want, then download the PDF. Print it at home or at a print shop, and keep it with your kit.

What to put in it

Where it comes from

Every guide is written in our own words. The facts come from Ready.gov, the CDC, FEMA, the American Red Cross, the USDA, university Extension and the other sources named under each guide. Each guide ends with its sources, and the back of the book lists every source with its web address.

Prepare Me is not part of, and is not endorsed by, any of these groups. To read a guide on screen instead, open Guides.
