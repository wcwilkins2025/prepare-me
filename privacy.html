Privacy

What is kept, who can see it, and how to make it go away. In plain words.

What is kept

Your email, to sign you in. Your answers: how many people live with you by age group, how many pets, yes or no on daily medicine and a medical device, the kind of home, own or rent, where your water comes from, and whether a vehicle runs. Your town and its county, with FEMA's ratings for that county. Each assessment and the scores it gave. Which steps you checked off and when. Your weekly check-in answers.

Not kept: your name, street address, phone number, documents or anything you own. Prepare Me never asks for them.

Who can see it

Only you. Every table has a rule in the database that lets a signed-in man read and change his own rows and nobody else's. There is no report of members, no list and no sharing. Nothing is posted to Skool or anywhere else.

Where it lives

In a Supabase database. The site itself is plain pages on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Links to Ready.gov and the others open those sites, which have their own privacy rules.

Taking it with you, or deleting it

On your account page: download everything as a file, or delete your account. Deleting removes your sign-in and every row, at once and for good.
