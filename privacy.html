Privacy

What is kept, who can see it, and how to make it go away. In plain words.

What is kept

Your email, to sign you in. Your answers: how many people live with you by age group, how many pets, yes or no on livestock or horses, daily medicine, a medical device and needing help to get around or leave fast, the kind of home, own or rent, where your water comes from, and whether a vehicle runs. Your town and its county, with FEMA's ratings for that county. Each assessment and the scores it gave. Which steps you checked off and when. Your weekly check-in answers. If you use the forum: the display name you pick, your posts, and any post you report.

If you turn on phone alerts: the address your phone's browser gives for alerts (it holds no message), your alert choices, your time zone so the check-in reminder comes at a sensible hour, and the alerts sent to you, for 60 days. The alert that reaches your phone is empty. Your phone then fetches what it says from Prepare Me, so the phone makers' alert services never see it. Weather alerts are the National Weather Service's own words for the county in your assessment.

If you write a family plan: what you type into it. That can be names, phone numbers, your home address, where you meet, where things are kept, and health needs. All of it is your choice, and every spot can be left blank.

Not kept: Social Security, bank, card or policy numbers, documents, or anything you own. Prepare Me never asks for them, and the plan says not to type them.

Who can see it

Only you. That includes your family plan. Every table has a rule in the database that lets a signed-in man read and change his own rows and nobody else's. There is no report of users, no list and no sharing. Nothing is posted anywhere outside Prepare Me.

The forum is the one exception, and only for what you write there. Your forum posts and your display name are seen by everyone signed in to Prepare Me. Your email never is. Visitors who are not signed in see nothing. The moderator can hide a post and reads reports; a report does not show who sent it to anyone else.

Where it lives

In a Supabase database. The site itself is plain pages on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal information. Links to Ready.gov and the others open those sites, which have their own privacy rules.

Taking it with you, or deleting it

On your account page: download everything as a file, or delete your account. Deleting removes your sign-in and every row, at once and for good, with your forum posts and the replies under your topics.
